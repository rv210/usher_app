Church Usher Ministry Platform

Privacy Policy

Application: Guardians of the Gate • Effective Date: September 16, 2026 • Version: 1.0.0

1 Introduction & Ministry Purpose

This Privacy Policy explains how Guardians of the Gate ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, protects, and handles personal and operational information when you use our church ushering management mobile application.

The App is dedicated strictly to facilitating church logistics, service attendance headcounts, usher station assignments, emergency accountability, and internal team communication.

2 Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to deliver and coordinate ministry operations:

  • User Account Information: Full name, church email address, and phone number provided during account registration and authentication.
  • Ministry & Operational Metrics: Station duty check-ins, service headcount tally numbers, and operational checklist submissions.
  • User-Selected Media: Profile photos or chat attachments that you explicitly choose to upload from your camera or photo gallery.
  • Authentication Credentials: Securely hashed sign-in tokens and verification IDs generated by Google Firebase Authentication.

3 Device Permissions & Usage

The App requests the following permissions solely for user-initiated functionality:

  • Photos & Media (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / Gallery Access): Used only when you choose to upload a profile photo or share sanctuary setup and station photos in team communications. Access is strictly on-demand and applies only to files you explicitly pick. The App never accesses your broader media library or scans background files.
  • Camera: Used solely when you choose to take a live photo for your profile avatar or team chat update.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to deliver critical ministry dispatch updates, emergency station notices, and schedule reminders.
  • Biometrics (USE_BIOMETRIC): Optionally used to securely unlock the application on your device using fingerprint or face recognition without storing biometric data on external servers.

4 How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to fulfill church operational requirements:

  • Authenticating your identity and providing two-step security verification.
  • Scheduling and dispatching ushers to church stations (e.g., Main Sanctuary, Vestibule, Doors).
  • Aggregating church attendance tallies for pastoral leadership and record-keeping.
  • Enabling real-time communication among church usher staff during worship services.

5 Zero Sale or Commercial Sharing of Data

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data. Your contact details and ministry activities are never disclosed to third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks.

6 Data Security & Cloud Infrastructure

All communications between the App and our backend are encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (SSL/TLS). Data is stored on Google Firebase Cloud infrastructure with strict Firestore security rules ensuring that only authorized church leaders and authenticated ushers can view ministry records.

7 Tester Privacy Shield

During app testing phases and Google Play closed testing tracks, the App implements a Tester Privacy Shield: live church member directories and personal contact lists are suppressed so that external community reviewers and testers never access private member information.

8 Data Retention & Account Deletion Rights

You have the right to access, update, or request the complete deletion of your user account and associated personal data at any time. You can submit an online request directly via our Account Deletion Request Page or email our ministry administration team at guardiansgates26@gmail.com. Requests are processed within 3 business days.

9 Children's Privacy

Guardians of the Gate is designed for church ministry volunteers and staff. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.
