User Privacy & Data Rights

Request Account & Data Deletion

Application: Guardians of the Gate • Effective Date: September 16, 2026

1 Overview & Policy Compliance

In accordance with Google Play Developer Data Safety policies and user privacy rights, users of Guardians of the Gate have the full right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal records.

You do not need to have the app installed on your phone to complete this request. You can submit your deletion request directly through this web page, or initiate it from within the app settings.

2 What Data is Permanently Deleted

Upon processing your account deletion request, the following data is permanently purged from our Firebase servers:

  • Account Credentials: Your Firebase Authentication user record, login credentials, and two-step verification phone linkage.
  • Personal Profile: Full name, church email, contact phone number, and avatar photo.
  • Ministry History: Station duty history, personal checklist logs, and assigned operational shifts.
  • Communications & Uploads: Chat messages, uploaded setup photos, and direct announcement acknowledgments.

What Data is Retained: Anonymized, aggregate sanctuary headcount tallies (e.g., total worshippers at a service) are retained for historical church operational metrics. These metrics contain zero identifying personal information.

3 Web Deletion Request Form

Submit this form to initiate deletion of your account. Our administration team will verify the request and permanently delete your account within 3 business days.

✓ Your deletion request has been submitted. A confirmation email has been dispatched, and your account records will be permanently purged within 3 business days.

4 Alternative: In-App Deletion Steps

If you currently have the app installed on your Android device, you may also delete your account instantly from within the application:

  1. Open Guardians of the Gate.
  2. Navigate to Settings (gear icon in the bottom or side navigation).
  3. Scroll to the Account & Security section.
  4. Tap Delete Account & Data.
  5. Confirm the prompt to immediately wipe your session and credentials.
